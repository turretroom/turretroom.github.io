About

My name is Anthony Tinaro. I created the Turret Room as a "house" for all my creative projects. I am an artist from Rhode Island who is passionate about making the world a better place. If I hope to improve the world, there must be room for the world in the Turret Room. For me, the world must have room for creativity and creatives must always make room for the creativity of others. I see my work as "building" something that exists outside of myself. Many artists take their individual personalities very seriously, which I respect, but I am very much aware of the fact that creativity itself is collective. That which is created belongs to everybody, even if the products which signify or carry the creative work are owned privately.

Not one man, woman, or person is responsible for culture - the people are! The only way this room gets larger is if you work to expand it. You can update, adapt, improve, and distribute anything you see here on this website.
