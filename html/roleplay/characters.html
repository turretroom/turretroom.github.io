Character Creation



  1. Roll for your Character's Passions. The Passions are Proportion, Beauty, Sublime, and Pathos. These are the four bonuses a character can add to many d20 Checks throughout the game.
  2. Roll for your character's Birth Signs. You should begin the game with a Star Sign, a Sun Sign, and a Moon Sign.
  3. Choose your Character's Allegiance. You can be loyal to Law, an agent of Chaos, or a judge of Neutrality, also called the Balance.
  4. Choose or roll your Character's Languages. Try to make sure every member of your party shares at least one Language with another character.
  5. Choose your Character's Prowess.
  6. Determine your starting Health by rolling your Character's Will.
  7. You have 110 Gold Pieces to purchase your Character's starting Equipment.
  8. Write down your Character's name, and think about their personality, motivations, and backstory as you play them.


Experience



Gather Experience from collecting and spending Treasure that you find in the dungeons and wilderness. Every time your character reaches one of the Experience Point totals above, they earn a level and progress on the pathway of their Passion. Treasure that is found and hoarded does not earn XP for characters. The referee will also award experience points when a character completes a quest, performs an exceptionally dramatic feat, and as a reward for good roleplaying. This is done at the end of each game session.
