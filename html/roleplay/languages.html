Common Signs



All characters are able to use a system of Signs to wordlessly communicate using gestures and body language to any creature that speaks a Language. There are three types of Common Signs: Sign, Touchtap, and Vibrablop. These are only used when communication happens between characters who do not share a language. They can be used to communicate any concept, with the risk of Miscommunication. Common Signs can be used at any time, even in Combat.



Common Sign Origin Form
Sign Overworld Hand gestures, body movements, facial expressions
Touchtap Underdeep Touch, tapping on surface of skin, tapping on nearby objects
Vibrablop Undersea Making vibrations in water with fins and gills


Time Spent Communicating Length of message Examples
1 Bout (10 Seconds) 2 Sentences "Don't go in there, it's dangerous!" "Please stop fighting us, let's talk instead!"
1 Round (10 Minutes) Up to 360 Sentences More complex negotiations over truces can take place. Both sides of the conversation are in a rush.
1 Turn (1 Hours) Unlimited A conversation can take place until the sun goes down.


Miscommunication

Any time a player character tries to use Common Signs to communicate at least two sentences, they roll a Sign Check. The result on a d20 must be at least 13. Characters communicating with some NPCs may need to roll higher at the referee's discretion. A character's Beauty may be added to this Check. The referee can decide the result of the Miscommunication or roll on the table for inspiration.

Allegiance Signs

These are more complex systems of Sign language developed by societies involved in the Psychic War. All characters that share the same Allegiance as another character can use Signs to communicate complex concepts without the chance of Miscommunication.



Deaf Characters

Deaf characters can always communicate with each other and the group of player character adventurers without any chance for Miscommunication.



Languages



Verbal and written communication is only possible when two characters share a language with each other. This communication is seamless and only requires the players and referee to roleplay interactions between characters and NPCs.

Your Sublime bonus adds to the number of Languages you can begin the game speaking, reading, and writing fluently from the Humanoid Languages table. You may choose one of your Humanoid Languages as your character's native tongue.

Any character that can speak the same Language as you can become your Companion.

Reaction Rolls

These are rolled by the referee away from the players. Upon meeting a group of player characters, all NPCs will have a Reaction. This reaction is random, but may be modified by several factors.

Reputation Character groups that are known for virtuous and noble deeds can earn up to a +3 bonus on Reaction Rolls. Character groups known for wicked deceit and brutal acts can earn up to a -3 penalty on [[P&K - Reaction Rolls]].

Language Characters that speak the same language may earn a bonus to [[P&K - Reaction Rolls]] when speaking with an NPC, while characters who speak a language from an enemy group to the NPC may have a penalty to [[P&K - Reaction Rolls]].

Allegiance Characters that share an Allegiance with other characters can earn up to a +3 bonus on Reaction Rolls.
